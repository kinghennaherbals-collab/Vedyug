About Vedyug Naturals

Since 2017, VEDYUG NATURALS has been dedicated to delivering premium-quality henna powders and essential oils crafted from pure and natural ingredients sourced from the rich lands of Sojat, Rajasthan.

We believe henna is not just a product — it is a tradition, culture, and emotion passed from generation to generation.

Our mission is to preserve the purity of traditional herbal henna while providing professional-grade quality for bridal artists, wholesalers, exporters, and private labeling businesses across India and globally.

With a commitment to quality, authenticity, and customer satisfaction, VEDYUG NATURALS has built a trusted identity in the herbal industry.
